English Phoneme Pronunciation Practice

Recording Controls

1. Click "Record" next to any phoneme
2. Speak the phoneme clearly
3. Click "Stop" when done
4. Use "Play" to listen to your recording

Tips for Recording

Use a quiet room with minimal background noise
Speak clearly and at a normal pace
Keep microphone about 6 inches from your mouth
Practice each phoneme multiple times

About Invalid Recordings

⚠️ Recordings marked as "Old format (lost)" are from an older version
that only saved temporary links, not actual audio data.
These recordings cannot be recovered - you'll need to re-record them.
Use the "Clean Up Invalid Recordings" button to remove them from your list.

Note

New recordings are stored as real audio data (Base64 format).
Export your recordings to save them permanently and share them.
Exported files will only contain valid recordings.

How to Use

Step 1: Type at least 3 letters in the word converter to see available words
Step 2: Select a word from the dropdown or type the full word
Step 3: Click "Convert & Add to Table" to add it to the Words table
Step 4: Click "Record" to record your pronunciation, then "Stop" when finished
Step 5: Use "Play" to listen back to your recording

Custom Word Dictionary

Place a phonemeDict.csv file in the same directory with format:
word,phonemes
Example: hello,h ɛ ˈl oʊ
If CSV is not found, the default dictionary will be used.
